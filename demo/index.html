THE FULL SELF-HOSTED PACKAGE

Self-hosted corporate EHS software

Autonomous EHS Management is environmental, health and safety software for incident management, corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), and human verification, with PostgreSQL as the system of record. Corporate EHS teams can follow reported issues through assigned ownership and recorded evidence.

The full application includes authentication, permission checks, and an audit trail. Optional AI can suggest wording; people review and close records. Module maturity varies. This public GitHub Pages walkthrough demonstrates synthetic, read-only records and does not replace the self-hosted application.

Source is available under the Apache License 2.0. The software does not claim OSHA certification and is not an official filing tool.
